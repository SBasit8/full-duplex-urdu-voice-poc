 How Moshi predicts text and audio together                                                                                                                             
   
  At every frame (every 80 ms = 12.5 Hz), the model emits 9 things in parallel:                                                                                          
                  
  Frame T:                                                                                                                                                               
     text_token         ← 1 token from 32k Urdu vocabulary (or padding)
     audio_code_lvl_0   ← 1 token from Mimi's first codebook (semantic info)                                                                                             
     audio_code_lvl_1   ← 1 token from Mimi's second codebook (acoustic detail)                                                                                          
     ...                                                                                                                                                                 
     audio_code_lvl_7   ← 1 token from Mimi's 8th codebook (fine acoustic detail)                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                         
  These 9 outputs are predicted by two transformers stacked together:                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                                         
                      ┌─────────────────────────────┐                                                                                                                    
                      │   Temporal Transformer      │
  Input frames    →   │   (the big "main" model)    │   →   hidden state                                                                                                 
                      │   processes seq across time │                                                                                                                    
                      └──────────────┬──────────────┘                                                                                                                    
                                     │                                                                                                                                   
                                     ↓                                                                                                                                   
                      ┌─────────────────────────────┐
                      │   Depth Transformer         │                                                                                                                    
                      │   predicts the 9 outputs    │   →   text + 8 audio
                      │   (text first, then audio)  │       codes for this frame                                                                                         
                      └─────────────────────────────┘                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                                         
  The Depth Transformer is what enforces alignment. It predicts the text token FIRST, then conditions all 8 audio codebook predictions on that text token. So in theory, 
  the audio for frame T is "told" what word the model thinks it's saying.
                                                                                                                                                                         
  What "alignment" should look like ideally

  If the model is well-trained, at frame 50 (say 4 sec in), you'd see:                                                                                                   
  - text_token = "مالس" (the word "hello" in Urdu)
  - audio_codes = encode the sounds of saying "مالس"                                                                                                                     
                                                    
  If you decode the audio, you'd hear "مالس". If you decode the text, you'd read "مالس". They match.    
  
  
  Why our model's text and audio don't match                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                                         
  Several things compound:                                                                                                                                               
  
  1. Text supervision is extremely sparse                                                                                                                                
                  
  Look at the format we trained on (per dialogue):                                                                                                                       
  Frame:        0    1    2    3    4    5    6    7   ...   100  101  ...  150  ...
  Text:        [3,   3,   3,   0,   42,  3,   3,   3,  ...,   3,   0,  ...,  31,  ...]                                                                                   
                ↑    ↑    ↑    ↑    ↑                              ↑                                                                                                     
                pad  pad  pad  end  word "مالس"                   word "پآ"                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                                         
  Token 3 = padding (silence frames). Token 0 = end-of-text marker. Real word tokens (like 42, 31) only appear at the exact frames where words START.                    
                                                                                                                                                                         
  Out of ~1500 frames per dialogue, only ~50-100 are real word tokens. The rest is padding.                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                                         
  So the model sees:                                                                                                                                                     
  - ~95% of frames: "predict pad/end-of-text" (boring, easy to learn)
  - ~5% of frames: "predict the right Urdu word" (hard, where actual learning happens)                                                                                   
                                                                                      
  This sparse supervision means the text stream gets ~20× less effective training signal per epoch than the audio stream (which has a meaningful target at every frame). 
                                                                                                                                                                         
  2. We re-initialized text embeddings from scratch                                                                                                                      
                                                                                                                                                                         
  When we ran init_moshi_for_ft.py --init_text_embeddings, we wiped the text embedding table (32K rows × ~4096 dims = ~130M parameters of Urdu word meanings). The model 
  must relearn:
  - "Token ID 47 means the sound 'فلا' (alif)"                                                                                                                           
  - "Token ID 1283 means the word 'ناتسکاپ'"                                                                                                                             
  - "Token ID 9412 means the morpheme 'ےہ'" 
                                                                                                                                                                         
  All from scratch. With only 250K real word occurrences in 38 hrs, each token is seen on average ~8 times during training — barely enough to memorize, let alone        
  generalize.                                                                                                                                                            
                                                                                                                                                                         
  For comparison: Kyutai's original English Moshi learned its text embeddings over hundreds of millions of word occurrences across thousands of hours.                   
                  
  3. Audio codebooks were retained, not reset                                                                                                                            
                  
  We kept Kyutai's audio codebook embeddings. So the model started training with strong audio knowledge already (the 8 Mimi codebooks were working from frame 0). The    
  text side started at random.
                                                                                                                                                                         
  This creates an asymmetry:                                                                                                                                             
  - Audio: 5 epochs of fine-tuning on top of strong base = decent quality
  - Text: 5 epochs of training from scratch = poor quality                                                                                                               
                  
  4. The two streams are weakly coupled in early training                                                                                                                
                  
  In theory, the Depth Transformer makes audio condition on text. In practice, when text predictions are mostly random (early training), the audio side learns to ignore 
  the text input and just predict from the temporal context alone.
                                                                                                                                                                         
  This is bad: it means audio and text become decoupled. Even when text starts to improve later, audio is already stuck in a "I don't trust the text" pattern.           
  
  Why this improves with more training (the recovery path)                                                                                                               
                  
  Architecture is sound — it's purely a data/training issue                                                                                                              
                  
  Moshi's design (joint prediction + Depth Transformer + delay pattern) is correct. Given enough training:                                                               
                  
  Epochs 1-5:    Text mostly random.      Audio uses temporal context only.    DECOUPLED                                                                                 
  Epochs 5-15:   Text starts to learn     Audio still mostly ignoring text.    PARTIAL                                                                                   
                 common Urdu words.                                                                                                                                      
  Epochs 15-30:  Text gets coherent.      Audio starts to use text again       COUPLING                                                                                  
                                          (because text is informative now).                                                                                             
  Epochs 30+:    Text high quality.       Audio strongly conditioned on text.  ALIGNED                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                         
  The "decoupled" → "aligned" transition is where the magic happens. It typically requires:                                                                              
  - Text loss to drop below ~3.0                                                                                                                                         
  - Many epochs of joint optimization                                                                                                                                    
  - Sufficient data variety          
                                                                                                                                                                         
  In your case (text loss = 3.75 at epoch 5):
  - You're in late "decoupled" / early "partial" zone                                                                                                                    
  - Text is starting to make sense (real Urdu words emerge!)                                                                                                             
  - But audio hasn't caught up to using it                                                                                                                               
                                                                                                                                                                         
 
  

  
  
  The alignment problem    :                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                         
  This is a known phenomenon in multimodal models:                                                                                                                       
  - Text and audio have very different statistical properties (text is sparse + discrete word events; audio is dense + continuous evolution)
  - They learn at different rates                                                                                                                                        
  - Early in training, they're mostly independent
  - A "phase transition" happens later when they become strongly coupled                                                                                                 
  - Models trained too briefly are stuck in the "independent" phase     
                                                                                                                                                                         
  J-Moshi explicitly mentions this in their paper — they had to train for ~36 hours on 128 V100s (= ~4600 GPU-hours) for the streams to fully align.                     
                                                                                                                                                                         
  You did ~3 hours on 2 GPUs (= 6 GPU-hours). You're at ~0.13% of J-Moshi's training compute. So expecting full alignment is unrealistic — but the fact that you see real
  Urdu words in the text stream is itself a strong signal that the architecture is working as intended.
